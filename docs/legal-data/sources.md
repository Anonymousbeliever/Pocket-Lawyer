## Source Inventory

### 1. Kenya Law

**Organization:** National Council for Law Reporting (Kenya Law)

**Official website:** https://new.kenyalaw.org/

**Authority level:** Primary / official legal source

**Material available:**
- Constitution
- Laws of Kenya
- Acts
- Subsidiary legislation
- Legal notices
- County legislation
- Treaties
- Judicial decisions
- Kenya Gazette
- Other legal publications

**Collection status:** Investigated

**Planned use:**
Primary source for the legal corpus, particularly legislation,
subsidiary legislation and case law.

**Notes:**
Kenya Law identifies itself as the official source of Law Reports
and Consolidated Laws of Kenya.

---

### 3. Supreme Court of Kenya

**Organization:** Supreme Court of Kenya

**Official website:** https://supremecourt.judiciary.go.ke/

**Authority level:** Official judicial source

**Material available:**
- Judgments
- Rulings
- Advisory opinions
- Court rules
- Practice directions

**Collection status:** Investigated

**Planned use:**
Supreme Court decisions and other authoritative Supreme Court
materials.

**Notes:**
The Supreme Court maintains a dedicated decisions database with
search and filtering by year and document type.

---

### 4. Parliament of Kenya

**Organization:** Parliament of Kenya

**Official website:** https://www.parliament.go.ke/

**Authority level:** Official government source

**Material available:**
- Bills
- Parliamentary documents
- Legislative materials
- Parliamentary reports

**Collection status:** To be investigated

**Planned use:**
Parliamentary and legislative history material where useful.

**Important:**
Bills and parliamentary proposals must not be treated as enacted
law unless their legal status is verified.
